# NHS England Operational Pressures Escalation Levels (OPEL) Framework 2024–2026

**Document ID:** `NHS-OPEL-2024-STD`  
**Source:** NHS England Urgent and Emergency Care (UEC) Operations  

## OPEL 1: Normal Operating Capacity
- Trigger: 4-hour A&E compliance >= 82% (breach rate <= 18%), general & acute bed occupancy < 88%, zero ambulance handover delays > 30 minutes.
- Actions: Maintain business-as-usual clinical flow, routine morning/afternoon board rounds, and proactive discharge planning via Hospital Discharge Hubs.

## OPEL 2: Moderate Pressure (Early Escalation)
- Trigger: 4-hour A&E compliance between 76% and 81.9% (breach rate 18.1% to 24.0%), bed occupancy 88%–91.9%, intermittent ambulance handovers 30–44 minutes.
- Actions: Open Same Day Emergency Care (SDEC) extended evening shifts, route minor injury / low-acuity presentations to Urgent Treatment Centres (UTC), and activate Single Point of Access (SPoA) community admission avoidance.

## OPEL 3: Severe Pressure (Major Surge Alert)
- Trigger: 4-hour A&E compliance between 65% and 75.9% (breach rate 24.1% to 35.0%), bed occupancy 92%–95.9%, or multiple 45-minute ambulance handover breaches, or UKHSA Amber Cold Health Alert coinciding with winter respiratory surge.
- Mandatory Actions: Convene Silver Executive Command twice daily, enforce the **45-minute maximum ambulance handover standard** with Rapid Assessment & Triage (RAT) and fit-to-sit cohorting, accelerate step-down transfers to **Virtual Wards** (targeting >= 80% Virtual Ward utilisation), and expand SDEC medical/surgical ambulatory pathways.

## OPEL 4: Critical / Potential Clinical Safety Compromise
- Trigger: 4-hour A&E compliance < 65% (breach rate > 35.0%), bed occupancy >= 96%, widespread 12-hour trolley waits (> 10% of attendances or > 40 patients waiting > 12h), or UKHSA Red Cold Health Alert with Trust-level critical capacity deficit.
- Mandatory Actions: Convene Gold Executive Command and Cheshire & Merseyside / Regional ICB System Control Centre (SCC), enact immediate regional ambulance diverts and mutual aid across peer Trusts within 25 km, cancel non-urgent elective inpatient activity to protect emergency flow, and invoke full NHS England OPEL 4 Critical Incident Escalation protocols.
