# NICE Guideline NG94: Emergency and Acute Medical Care in Over 16s

**Document ID:** `NICE-NG94-UEC`  
**Source:** National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE)  

## Service Delivery & Early Senior Review
- **Early Consultant Review:** Ensure patients admitted as medical emergencies are assessed by an acute medicine consultant or senior decision-maker within 4 hours of admission or 14 hours of arrival to prevent prolonged Emergency Department trolley stays.
- **Same Day Emergency Care (SDEC):** Provide dedicated ambulatory SDEC units operating at least 12 hours a day, 7 days a week for appropriately triaged low- and moderate-risk patients (e.g., cellulitis, DVT, PE rule-out, stable respiratory exacerbations) so they avoid overnight acute bed stays.

## Multidisciplinary Flow, Discharge Planning & Virtual Wards
- **Discharge Coordination from Admission:** Start discharge planning at the point of hospital presentation ("Home First" / Discharge to Assess), utilizing community intermediate care and **Virtual Wards** (Hospital at Home) for remote oximetry and respiratory step-down monitoring.
- **Standardised Bed Management & Capacity Forecasting:** Use real-time predictive demand analytics integrating local weather cold-snap alerts and weekly pathogen incidence (Influenza, RSV, Norovirus) to trigger proactive medical bed reconfigurations before Monday morning peak demand.
- **Parametric Safety Monitoring:** Track 12-hour decision-to-admit waits and acute medical unit (AMU) turnaround times continuously to mitigate avoidable inpatient mortality associated with ED crowding.
