# RCEM Safe Staffing, Crowding & Urgent Care Standards

**Document ID:** `RCEM-CROWDING-2024`  
**Source:** Royal College of Emergency Medicine (RCEM)  

## Crowding Harms & 12-Hour Trolley Wait Limits
- Emergency department crowding directly correlates with increased 30-day standardised mortality when patient stays exceed 6 to 12 hours awaiting an inpatient bed.
- **12-Hour Wait Cap:** Trusts must drive stays over 12 hours from arrival down below 2% (interim cap < 10%) and prevent exit block by guaranteeing flow out of resus and majors cubicles.
- **NHS 78% 4-Hour Standard:** Achieve at least 78% of patients admitted, transferred, or discharged within 4 hours across all Type 1/2/3 attendances (keeping the 4-hour breach rate <= 22%).

## Safe Staffing Ratios & Ambulance Handover Protocol
- **45-Minute Maximum Ambulance Handover Standard:** No patient arriving by emergency ambulance should wait longer than 45 minutes for clinical handover; cohorting or corridor care must trigger formal Executive Risk Assessment and OPEL 3 escalation.
- **Triage & Initial Assessment Within 15 Minutes:** Every self-presenting Type 1 A&E patient must receive structured clinical triage within 15 minutes of arrival.
- **Safe Consultant & Nursing Establishment:** Minimum 1:1 registered nurse staffing in Resuscitation bays, 1:4 in Majors, and continuous Emergency Medicine Consultant shop-floor presence during 08:00–24:00 peak arrival windows (especially Monday Morning Peaks and Winter Respiratory Surges).
